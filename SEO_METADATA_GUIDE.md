## 🔍 SEO Best Practices Applied

### Title Tag Guidelines
- ✅ Under 60 characters for full display in SERPs
- ✅ Primary keyword at the beginning
- ✅ Brand name included for recognition
- ✅ Compelling call-to-action language
- ✅ No keyword stuffing

### Meta Description Guidelines
- ✅ Under 160 characters for full display
- ✅ Includes primary keywords naturally
- ✅ Persuasive benefit-focused copy
- ✅ Clear call-to-action
- ✅ Unique value propositions highlighted

### Brand Voice Consistency
- **Tone**: Professional, trustworthy, local expertise
- **Keywords**: Himachal, tours, packages, taxi services, spiritual, adventure
- **USPs**: Local experts, honest pricing, on-ground support, custom itineraries
- **CTAs**: Book today, Plan your trip, Start planning, Explore with us

## 🚀 Additional SEO Recommendations

### Structured Data
The home page already includes JSON-LD structured data for Organization and WebSite. Consider adding:

1. **LocalBusiness Schema** for Chintpurni location
2. **TravelAgency Schema** for services
3. **TouristTrip Schema** for individual packages

### Internal Linking
- Link from Home to Packages with keyword-rich anchor text
- Link from About to Contact with "Get in touch" or "Plan your trip"
- Cross-link related packages within the Packages page

### Image Alt Text
Ensure all images have descriptive alt text including location names and relevant keywords.
